# Cue — Chromatic Atelier

## Creative intent

Bring together two visual registers: the engineered precision of a motion tool and the care of a printed drawing book. The interface should feel contemporary and expressive, while its framing and line work nod to engraving, illumination, and print registration.

The traditional reference is a material and craft language, not a historical costume. The futuristic reference comes from controlled shader motion, index marks, and technical hierarchy, not neon gradients or glass panels.

## Color system: 60 / 25 / 15

| Share | Color | Use |
|---:|---|---|
| 60% | Mineral parchment `#F0ECE2` | Main canvas, writing surfaces, breathing room |
| 25% | Plum `#403248` / aubergine `#352A3D` | Navigation rail, hero headers, image-stage frames, lab identity |
| 15% | Saffron `#E5B44F` | Primary actions, active state, engraved highlights, small ornaments |

Dark interface text uses aubergine charcoal `#2A2530`, avoiding pure black. The warm/cool pair stays consistent: saffron actions sit on plum or parchment, while the plum surfaces carry pale text. The ratio is a visual composition guide; component contrast still needs measured checks before production.

## Visual craft

- **Traditional layer:** double rules, restrained geometric rosettes, etched portrait lines, paper tone, editorial serif headlines.
- **Future layer:** technical labels, frame indices, cursor-reactive Wave-Line shader, precise timeline cells, compact mono metadata.
- **Product layer:** actual drawings remain the focus; controls and status are ordinary semantic UI, not canvas-only content.
- **Shape language:** broad rectangular stages, thin ornamental borders, small circular controls only where the meaning calls for them.
- **Motion:** slow line drift in the isolated lab; workflow transitions stay short and purposeful.

## Screen art direction

1. **Sign in:** half-page plum cover, warm etched portrait, gold rule work, quiet account form.
2. **Library:** plum editorial title panel with a geometric line seal; the work archive stays on a light ground.
3. **Keyframes:** two equal drawing plates in plum frames; A/B and the connector are easy to scan.
4. **Motion plan:** colored title plate then a two-column review: English prompt, ordered movement cues, explicit approval.
5. **Generation:** status is announced in plum/gold; the sixteen frame cells show activity without implying a percentage.
6. **Result:** large playback stage with a visible frame index and metadata set back in the light surface.
7. **Wave-Line lab:** a dedicated plum title panel, parchment/saffron shader field, and four editable controls.

## Shader use

The lab converts a sample drawing's grayscale tone to wavy line weight. Parameters are isolated from the workflow. The effect is not applied to user-uploaded drawings or generated frames, so the mockup does not imply a transformation Cue does not promise.

The canvas is capped at a 1.5 pixel ratio and 30 frames per second, stops when leaving the lab or when the tab is hidden, and renders a still image under reduced-motion preference. HTML remains responsible for labels, navigation, and controls.

- [Pixelismo / AVAU](https://www.pixelismo.it/avau.html): cohesive world-building and material shifts.
- [Codrops sketch post-processing](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2022/11/29/sketchy-pencil-effect-with-three-js-post-processing/): optional future NPR technique study.
- [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/), [Mesh3D](https://www.mesh3d.gallery/), and [Shadertoy](https://www.shadertoy.com/): art direction and shader experimentation references.

Upload constraints, final plan schema, and model metadata remain open.
