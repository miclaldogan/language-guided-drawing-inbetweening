# Visual Critique of V2 (Prismatic Paper)

Evidence: the V2 renders in `../v2-prismatic-paper/previews/` (S1–S6, S2 and S4 in A and B) and the two V2 style tiles, viewed as images. The colours were not the problem; the art direction was.

| # | Observation (what is visible) | Why it reads as unprofessional | V3 response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One saturated gold covers 33–43% of every screen: nav, title zone, margins, gaps. Wordmark and nav sit on it with only a thin rule. | The brand colour is wallpaper, so it can't signal anything. Nav, page and surrounding space merge; hierarchy is flat; the eye gets no resting place. | Gold becomes a ground that frames paper sheets (S1–S4) or an accent (S5–S6). A deep purple nav separates chrome from content. Measured gold per screen 0–29%. |
| 2 | Logo and nav are ink on gold, no bar, no active-state structure. | Looks like an unfinished page header, not an application frame. | Dedicated 64 px purple nav, cream wordmark with gold end ticks, separators, gold active indicator, account control. |
| 3 | Every group is a cream rectangle with a 1.5 px black outline and a hard shadow (S2 grid, S3 slots, S4 panels, S6 sidebar). | Component-demo look. Borders carry all the grouping, so nothing is more important than anything else. | One sheet per screen; inside it, hairlines, spacing and tonal steps. Three border levels (none, hairline, strong for selection and focus). |
| 4 | Fraunces at 46 px as the title of every operational screen, plus 26 px serif panel headings. | Reads as an illustrated landing page. Serif competes with the work. | Fraunces only for the S1 hero, wordmark and printed plan numerals. Titles are Manrope 28. |
| 5 | The background is flat gold with grain. | No composition; empty space is just empty. | Registration-mark pattern in the ground margins only. |
| 6 | The same cartoon face (round head, dot eyes) appears in every thumbnail, keyframe and the hero. | Elementary drawing tutorial. Also undermines the claim that thumbnails are different user works. | Six stylised line studies (profile, three-quarter, front, two hair types, flips) with ink line and one flat misregistered pigment shape. |
| 7 | Chunky chevrons, thick elbow arrow and boxed lanes in the CueSheet. | Decorative arrows obscure the sequence; lanes imply timing. | Vertical rail with numbered nodes and the word "then"; speed as a three-bar meter plus a word; explicit "Order and speed only" caption. |
| 8 | Heavy dark buttons with offset shadows; chips with outlines. | Toy-like. | Flat purple primary, hairline secondary, tinted borderless chips. |
| 9 | S5 and S6 stay fully gold. | S5 should feel calm; S6 should put the video first. | Paper ground, one sheet, dark viewer for video and film strip. |

## Verdict
V2 used the right palette at the wrong proportions and with the wrong grammar (outline every box, big serif everywhere, repeat one illustration). V3 keeps the colour family and the two Cue signatures, changes the grammar.
